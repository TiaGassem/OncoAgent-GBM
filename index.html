OncoAgent-GBM: a free, open-source toolkit for glioblastoma drug discovery

Dock any drug into any protein and check the set-up with a redocking test, analyse gene-expression and survival data, screen compounds, fit IC50 curves and ask your PDFs questions with page-cited answers. Built by a neuroscience Master's student, tested automatically, and honest about its limits.

Try the live appSource code (MIT)

What you can do

  • Molecular docking with validation: AutoDock Vina, labelled 3D view, pocket residues and distances, automatic redocking test.
  • Omics: differential expression, volcano plot, PCA, gene-set enrichment, co-expression (works on your own table or demo data).
  • Survival statistics: Kaplan-Meier curves and log-rank test; docking enrichment (ROC AUC).
  • Compound screening: drug-likeness, brain-penetration rules, PAINS alerts.
  • IC50 fitting (four-parameter logistic) and a lab notebook.
  • Chat with papers: verbatim quotes with page numbers, no invented text.
  • A built-in guide chat that builds a step-by-step plan for beginners.

Frequently asked questions

How do I check that a docking set-up is reliable?

Use redocking: take a crystal structure with a bound drug, remove the drug, dock it back and measure the RMSD. A top pose within 2.0 Å of the crystal pose is the standard pass. OncoAgent-GBM runs this test automatically and includes a built-in example (imatinib in Abl kinase, PDB 1IEP, RMSD about 0.9 Å in our test).

Can I dock any drug into any protein?

Yes. Provide a SMILES and a protein structure (PDB ID or file); the app prepares both and runs AutoDock Vina. Validate each new protein with the redocking test before trusting scores.

How do I find differentially expressed genes between tumour and normal samples?

Upload a genes-by-samples table and group labels (or try the simulated demo data) in the Omics & Tools tab. The app reports log2 fold change with Benjamini-Hochberg FDR, a volcano plot and PCA. For raw RNA-seq counts use DESeq2 or edgeR.

How do I run a Kaplan-Meier survival curve and log-rank test online?

Upload a CSV with time, event (1 or 0) and group, or try the built-in textbook example (Freireich leukaemia data, chi-square 16.79), in the Statistics tab.

Can I chat with my PDFs and get page numbers?

Yes. Chat with Papers returns verbatim quotes from your PDFs with file name and page number. It never writes new sentences, so it cannot invent facts.

Is OncoAgent-GBM free and is it a medical device?

It is free and open source (MIT licence). It is for research and education only, not a medical device, and gives no medical advice.

Limits (please read)

Research and education only; not a medical device. Docking scores depend on preparation, box and seed, so validate every new protein. The ADME/toxicity screen is a local approximation, not SwissADME or ProTox-3. Target prediction is not built in. See the credibility notes in the repository.
